# APEX DENTAL & WELLNESS CLINIC - PATIENT FAQ & APPOINTMENT GUIDE

## 1. CLINIC OPERATING HOURS & LOCATION
- Address: 450 Medical Center Boulevard, Suite 300, Austin, Texas 78701.
- Clinic Hours: Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Saturday from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Closed on Sundays.
- Emergency On-Call Dental Line: Available 24/7 for severe tooth pain, trauma, or bleeding at (512) 555-0199.

## 2. COMMON TREATMENTS & PRICING
- Comprehensive Dental Exam & Digital X-Rays: $120 (Covered 100% by most PPO insurances).
- Routine Teeth Cleaning (Prophylaxis): $95 for adults, $75 for children under 12.
- In-Office Professional Laser Teeth Whitening: $350 (Includes take-home touch-up maintenance kit).
- Porcelain Dental Crowns: $850 to $1,100 per tooth depending on material.
- Clear Aligners (Invisalign): Full treatment packages range from $3,200 to $4,800 with 0% interest monthly financing options.

## 3. INSURANCE & PAYMENT POLICIES
- In-Network Insurance Providers: Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian, and Blue Cross Blue Shield.
- Self-Pay Patients: 10% discount on total treatment cost when paying in full via cash or debit card on the day of service.
- Payment Plans: Flexible 6, 12, or 24-month financing available through CareCredit and Sunbit with instant approval.

## 4. APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING & CANCELLATION RULES
- New Patients: Please arrive 15 minutes prior to your scheduled time to complete medical history intake forms.
- Cancellation Policy: We require at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment without penalty.
- Late Cancellations / Missed Appointments: A $50 missed appointment fee will be applied to late cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance.

## 5. POST-PROCEDURE CARE GUIDELINES
- After Tooth Extraction: Bite down gently on the gauze pad for 45 minutes. Avoid using straws, smoking, or vigorous spitting for 48 hours to prevent dry socket.
- After Local Anesthesia: Avoid chewing hot foods or solid items until the numbness completely wears off (typically 2 to 3 hours).
