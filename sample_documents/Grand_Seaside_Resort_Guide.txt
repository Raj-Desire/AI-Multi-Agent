# GRAND SEASIDE RESORT & SPA - GUEST INFORMATION & SERVICE GUIDE

## 1. ROOM TYPES & PRICING
- Deluxe Ocean View Suite: $280 per night. Features a king-size plush bed, private balcony overlooking the ocean, deep soaking marble bathtub, and high-speed Wi-Fi.
- Executive Garden Villa: $420 per night. Features a private plunge pool, two bedrooms, personal butler service, complimentary minibar, and direct botanical garden access.
- Standard King Room: $175 per night. Includes a king bed, 55-inch smart TV, work desk, and city-side balcony.

## 2. CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT POLICIES
- Standard Check-In Time: 3:00 PM (15:00).
- Standard Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM (11:00).
- Early Check-In: Available upon request from 10:00 AM for an additional fee of $50, subject to room availability.
- Late Check-Out: Available until 2:00 PM for $40, or until 6:00 PM at 50% of the nightly room rate.

## 3. DINING & RESTAURANTS
- The Azure Horizon (Fine Dining Seafood): Open daily for dinner from 6:30 PM to 10:30 PM. Smart casual dress code required. Reservations are strongly recommended.
- Palm Court All-Day Cafe: Open 24/7. Serves international buffet breakfast from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM ($35 per guest, or included in Bed & Breakfast packages).
- Sunset Poolside Bar: Open 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Happy hour daily between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM with buy-one-get-one cocktails.

## 4. SPA & RECREATIONAL AMENITIES
- Lotus Wellness Spa: Open 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Offers deep tissue massages ($120/hour), hot stone therapy, and aromatherapy facials.
- Infinity Swimming Pool: Open 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Towels provided poolside.
- 24-Hour Fitness Gym: Fully equipped with Peloton bikes, free weights, and treadmills. Keycard access required.

## 5. PET POLICY
- Dogs and cats under 30 lbs are welcome in Deluxe Ocean View and Villa categories.
- A one-time non-refundable pet cleaning sanitation fee of $65 per stay applies.
- Pets must be kept on a leash in public lobby areas and are not permitted in the swimming pool area.

## 6. TRANSPORTATION & AIRPORT TRANSFERS
- Airport Shuttle: Complimentary round-trip airport shuttle runs every 30 minutes between 6:00 AM and 11:00 PM.
- Private Luxury Sedan Transfer: Available at $75 one-way upon 24-hour advance booking.
- Valet Parking: Available at the main entrance for $25 per night. Free self-parking is available in the underground garage.

## 7. CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY
- Flexible Rates: Free cancellation up to 48 hours prior to the 3:00 PM check-in date. Cancellations made within 48 hours incur a charge equal to the first night's room rate.
- Non-Refundable Promotional Bookings: 100% deposit charged at time of booking with zero refund upon cancellation.
